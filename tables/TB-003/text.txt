Schedule 2: Payment milestones
1. This Schedule sets out the milestones at which the Contractor may apply for payment under clause 4 of the
Contract. Each application shall be accompanied by the evidence named in the Payment trigger column.
2. The Contract Administrator shall certify each milestone within 10 working days of a complete application. The
Council shall pay a certified amount within 30 days of receiving a valid invoice, in accordance with section 68 of the
Procurement Act 2023 and the Contract.
3. Retention of 3 per cent applies to milestones 1 to 10. Half of the retention is released at practical completion and
the balance at the end of the defects period.
4. Amounts exclude VAT. Milestones may be re-sequenced only by a variation instructed in writing.
5. An application shall be made on the Council's application form and shall state the milestone number, the sum
applied for and the evidence enclosed. An application without the evidence named in the Payment trigger column is
not a complete application, and the Contract Administrator shall say so in writing within 5 working days.
6. The due date for payment of a certified milestone is the date on which the Council receives a valid invoice for it.
The final date for payment is 30 days after the due date.
7. The Council shall issue a payment notice within 5 days of the due date, stating the sum it considers due and how
that sum is calculated. If the Council intends to pay less than the notified sum, it shall issue a pay less notice no later
than 7 days before the final date for payment, giving its reasons.
8. If a certified amount is not paid by the final date for payment, simple interest is payable on it at 8 per cent a year
above the Bank of England base rate from the final date for payment until the date of payment.
9. Invoices shall be addressed to the Council's Accounts Payable team, quote the contract reference and the
milestone number, and be submitted through the Council's e-invoicing portal. Paper invoices are not accepted.
10. Payment of a milestone is not evidence that the work is complete or free from defects and does not affect the
Contract Administrator's powers under clause 17 of the Contract.
11. Where a milestone is achieved in part only, the Contract Administrator may certify a part payment in proportion to
the value of the work completed, on the Contractor's written request.
12. The Council may deduct from a payment under this Schedule any sum the Contractor owes it under the Contract,
provided the deduction is stated in its payment notice or pay less notice.
13. The Contractor shall not assign the right to receive payment under this Schedule without the Council's prior written
consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
14. The amounts in the table below are fixed. They are not subject to fluctuation, indexation or any other adjustment
except by a variation under clause 22 of the Contract.
