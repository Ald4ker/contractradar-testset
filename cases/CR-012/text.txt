Contract award notice
Procurement Act 2023, section 50 · Notice reference 001206-2026 · Published 2026-01-22

1. Summary
Eastmere and Calder District Council (the Council), of Council Offices, Calder Way, Eastmere, EM5 3LD, gives notice
that on 2026-01-19 it awarded a contract for Emergency Flood Response and Debris Clearance (the Council's
reference ECDC/EMG/2026/02) to Ironside Waste Management Ltd, company number 07114290, of Ironside Depot,
Quarry Road, Harrowdale, HD9 1WM.

2. Procedure and justification
The contract was awarded by direct award under section 41 of the Procurement Act 2023 on the ground of extreme
and unavoidable urgency following the flooding of the Calder valley on 12 and 13 January 2026. The Council
considered that the goods and services were required urgently to protect life and property and that running a
competitive tendering procedure was not possible in the time available. One supplier was approached and 1 quotation
was received. A transparency notice was published on 2026-01-16.

3. Contract
The contract is a services contract. The relevant CPV codes are 90470000 (Cleaning services) and 90700000
(Environmental services). The estimated value of the contract, excluding VAT, is £420,000. The contract runs from
2026-01-20 to 2026-07-19 with no option to extend, a maximum term of 6 months. Payment terms are 30 days from
receipt of a valid invoice.

4. Standstill
No standstill period applies to this award because the contract was awarded by direct award under section 41 on
grounds of urgency. This notice was published on 2026-01-22.

5. Contact
Enquiries to tenders@eastmere-calder.example.gov. Signed: R. Hughes, Director of Resources.
