Officer decision record
Harrowdale District Council · Parks and Open Spaces · Decision ref ODR/2026/0142

Decision
To enter into Deed of Variation No. 1 to contract HDC-PKS-2021-07 (Provision of Grounds Maintenance Services for
Parks and Open Spaces) with Fenwick Grounds Maintenance Ltd (company number 06645928), extending the term
by 12 months to 31st March 2027 and increasing the contract sum from £1,550,000.00 to £1,860,000.00 (an increase
of £310,000.00) to reflect the additional 14 hectares of open space transferred from Harrowdale Parish Council.

Background
The contract was awarded following an open procedure on 11th February 2021 and entered into on 12th March 2021
for an initial term of 60 months from 1st April 2021 with no option to extend. The contract therefore expired on 31st
March 2026. The supplier has continued to deliver the service since that date on the existing terms pending
re-procurement, which has been delayed by the service review.

Procurement advice
The Head of Procurement advises that the variation is treated as a non-substantial modification under section
74(1)(b) of the Procurement Act 2023 on the basis that the overall nature of the contract is unchanged. No contract
change notice was published before the variation was agreed. A contract change notice will be considered once the
re-procurement timetable is confirmed.

Decision taken
Decision taken by R. Hughes, Director of Resources on 15th May 2026.
